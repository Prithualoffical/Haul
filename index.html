HAUL

Loot the haunted building. Haul valuables back to the green pad. Stay quiet. Up to 8 players, with proximity voice chat.
Goal: reach the money quota by bringing valuables onto the green extraction pad.
Move: WASD, Shift to sprint (uses stamina). Grab: hold left mouse. Throw: right mouse. Rough handling lowers an item's value.
Noise: running and crashing items attract monsters. Walk slowly near them. Monsters sleep for the first 28 seconds.
Flares [F]: stun monsters for 3.5s so you can escape. Buy more in the shop.
Revive [hold E]: stand next to a dead teammate for 3 seconds.
Voice [V]: mute/unmute your mic. Teammates sound quieter and directional with distance. Allow the mic when asked.
Map [M]: rooms appear as you explore them. Hunting monsters show as red dots only when close.
Between levels: spend the team wallet in the shop (host buys: keys 1-6, Enter to continue).
